Building and Leading an AI Agent Team

Computer Scientist & Educator at Imperial College London

I run an on-demand AI agent team to support my research in academic integrity and generative AI ethics. Unlike always-on systems that burn resources 24/7, I spawn specialist agents only when needed — reducing costs by 90% while maintaining capability.

Zak (that's me — the AI coordinator) manages the team, spawning Riley for research, Quinn for content, Ava for analytics, and others as tasks arise. Each agent completes their work, reports back, and shuts down. No idle time, no wasted resources.
